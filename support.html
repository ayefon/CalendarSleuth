Support

Getting started, common questions, and how to get in touch.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later, or a Mac running macOS 26 or later
  • A Tradier API token for market data (a free sandbox token gives delayed data; a production token comes with a Tradier brokerage account)
  • Optional: a free Alpha Vantage API key, for earnings dates

Getting Started

1. Add your Tradier token

Open Settings → Tradier, choose the Account type that matches your token (Sandbox or Production), and paste the token. It’s stored only in your device’s Keychain.

2. Enter a symbol

Type a symbol in the field at the top of the list and press Return. Calendar Sleuth fills in a spread to start from: a call, the first expiration 60 or more days out for the long, the Friday of the first week 5 or more days out for the short, and the strike nearest the stock listed for both.

3. Adjust the spread

Every choice is a picker: Call or Put, either expiration, or the strike. The delta button beside the strike (“20Δ”) picks the strike nearest your target delta on the short’s chain. Each change is checked again right away.

4. Read the results

This Window shows the net per contract to the short’s expiration; Outlook the price band, the projected break-even and the best net return; Week by Week every later window to the long’s expiration.

5. Not ready for a token yet?

Tap Try with Sample Data in the list (or in Settings → Tradier) to explore six symbols with made-up quotes, option chains and earnings dates.

FAQ

It says “Add your Tradier API token”, or Tradier rejected the token

Check that the Account setting matches the token: sandbox tokens only work with Sandbox, and production tokens only with Production. Use the eye button in Settings to check the saved token for typos.

“Tradier has no quote” for a symbol

Check the spelling. Calendar Sleuth covers what Tradier carries: US stocks and funds with listed options. Share classes need their class letter, for example BRK.B.

“Has no listed options 5 and 60 days out”

The symbol needs an expiration at least 5 days away for the short and one at least 60 days away for the long.

“No option is listed at the strike”

That strike isn’t listed for one of the two expirations. Pick another strike; the picker lists only strikes listed for both.

What does “net per contract” mean?

The short’s premium (its mid, counting only its extrinsic value) less the long’s decay over the same window and the commission, for one contract of 100 shares, at the short’s expiration if the stock is still at the last price. Above zero, the short’s premium covers the long’s decay.

What is the price band?

The range of stock prices over which this window’s short still covers the long’s decay. It’s centered on the strike, not on the stock, and each later week’s band is narrower. It isn’t a forecast or a probability.

What are the shaded and outlined bands on the chart?

The stock’s supply and demand zones: places where price paused for one to six candles before leaving sharply. Demand zones (price rallied away) use the up-candle color; supply zones (price dropped away) the down-candle color. Dashed outlines labeled W are the weekly chart’s zones. Yellow circles mark where price came back to a zone, and fainter zones are weak: price didn’t leave them cleanly. The switch above the chart turns them off for the symbol you’re viewing, and Settings → Chart sets how each symbol starts. They show where price reacted before, not where it will, and don’t change any figure.

Some weeks say “not listed yet”

Weekly options are usually listed only about six weeks ahead. For a symbol with weeklies, Calendar Sleuth fills in the later Fridays and prices them at the implied volatility the listed options point to.

The figures look off, or jump between checks

Option quotes are only fresh during market hours, and a wide bid/ask moves the mid every figure starts from. Tap Refresh, and check the bid and ask under the Long and Short rows.

No earnings dates

Add a free Alpha Vantage key in Settings → Earnings Dates. If the free tier’s daily lookups are used up, Outlook says so; dates come back the next day.

What’s kept if I delete the app?

On an iPhone or iPad, deleting the app erases your recent and followed spreads and your settings on that device. Your Tradier token and Alpha Vantage key are kept in the Keychain and usually come back after reinstalling; tap Remove in Settings first if you don’t want that. On the Mac, deleting the app keeps everything.

Does the app place trades?

No. Calendar Sleuth only reads market data. It never places orders, reads account balances or positions, or moves money.
