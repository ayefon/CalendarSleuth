Calendar Sleuth

Calendar spread analysis for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

What It Does

Calendar Sleuth looks at a calendar spread found in the option chain data: a long call or put that expires later, usually 60 to 90 days out, and a short one at the same strike that expires sooner, often next Friday.

For each short window it answers one question: does the short’s premium still cover the long’s time decay over the same window? Then it follows the spread week by week to the long’s expiration and shows the window where the answer turns to no. Every figure holds the stock and the implied volatilities where they are today; Calendar Sleuth makes no predictions or recommendations.

Features

Calls and puts • Net per contract for this window: the short’s extrinsic premium less the long’s Black-Scholes decay and commission • The price band: the stock prices between which the short still covers the decay • Week by Week to the long’s expiration, with forward implied volatilities and the projected break-even window • Net return on the long’s value, so far and per year • Earnings dates marked in the week they fall in (with a free Alpha Vantage key) • Ex-dividend dates, declared or expected, marked the same way (with a Tradier Brokerage token) • A chart with the strike and the band’s edges, and a strip under it to scroll back up to two years • Supply and demand zones on the chart, with the weekly zones outlined and the nearest supply and demand zones named under it • A target-delta strike button, and arrows under the chart to step the strike • Each leg’s bid and ask • Your last 50 spreads, and spreads you follow on the long’s starting value, with the price band drawn week by week • A log for a followed spread: the shorts and the long as you record them, the net so far beside what was projected • Sample data to try the app without an account

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later, or a Mac running macOS 26 or later. Market data needs your own Tradier API token (a free sandbox token works, with delayed data). Earnings dates need a free Alpha Vantage API key (optional).

Read-Only by Design

The app only reads market data: quotes, option chains and price history. It never places orders, reads account balances or positions, or moves money. Spreads are examples found in the option chain data, not recommendations. Calendar Sleuth is an analysis tool, not investment advice.
